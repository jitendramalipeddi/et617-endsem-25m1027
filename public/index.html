📋 Task Scenario: Article IV Language Policy Deliberation

Current Rule 4.1: "All students must speak exclusively in English at all times across all school premises, including classrooms, corridors, sports field, mess, and bus."

Learner Context: In our school, many students speak Telugu at home and are learning English as their medium of education. During lunch, playground games, and breaks, students often feel constrained, anxious, or unable to freely express themselves when forced to speak only English.

Your Mission as a Group: Deliberate whether the rule should be modified so that English is mandatory only inside the classroom during lessons, while allowing home languages (like Telugu) in informal spaces (playground, mess, common areas) with fairness and inclusion for all students. Work together to propose and agree upon a revised clause!
